Fabric & Care

Materials & Care

Every piece is made to order using premium blanks, printed or embroidered and finished individually. Compositions vary slightly by product below.

Tees

  • 100% cotton
  • Fabric weight: 5.0–5.3 oz/yd² (170–180 g/m²)
  • Pre-shrunk jersey knit
  • Taped neck and shoulders
  • Double seam at sleeves and bottom hem

White may appear off-white rather than bright white due to the natural fabric.

Outreach Hoodies

  • 65% ring-spun cotton, 35% polyester · 100% cotton face
  • Fabric weight: 8.5 oz/yd² (288 g/m²)
  • Front pouch pocket · 3-panel hood
  • Matching flat drawstrings

Coordinates Hoodies

  • 100% organic cotton
  • Fabric weight: 10.32 oz/yd² (350 g/m²) — heavyweight
  • Self-fabric double-layered hood · front pouch pocket
  • GOTS & OCS certified organic · OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 · PETA-Approved Vegan

Polos

  • 100% combed ring-spun cotton
  • Fabric weight: 6.2 oz/yd² (210 g/m²)
  • Ribbed collar · tone-on-tone 3-button placket
  • Straight hem with side slits

Care

  • Machine wash cold, inside out, with like colours
  • Do not iron directly on the print or embroidery
  • Tumble dry low or hang to dry
  • Do not dry clean or bleach

Following these steps keeps your piece looking sharp for longer.
