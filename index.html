Fabric & Care

Materials & Care

Every piece is made to order using premium blanks, printed or embroidered and finished individually. Compositions vary slightly by product.

Tees & Crops

  • 100% cotton
  • Fabric weight: 5.0–5.3 oz/yd² (170–180 g/m²)
  • Pre-shrunk jersey knit
  • Taped neck and shoulders

White may appear off-white rather than bright white due to the natural fabric.

Washed Hoodie

  • 80% organic combed ring-spun cotton, 20% recycled polyester
  • Brushed 3-end fleece with a 100% organic cotton face
  • Fabric weight: 9.7 oz/yd² (330 g/m²) — heavyweight
  • Garment-dyed, washed finish — shading varies slightly piece to piece
  • Relaxed fit · kangaroo pocket

Washed Sweatshirt

  • Shell: 80% organic ring-spun combed cotton, 20% recycled polyester
  • Brushed 3-end fleece with a 100% organic cotton face
  • Fabric weight: 9.7 oz/yd² (330 g/m²) — heavyweight
  • Garment-dyed, washed finish — shading varies slightly piece to piece
  • Relaxed fit · elastane-free 1×1 rib at the neck, cuffs and hem

Heavyweight Sweatshirt

  • 80% organic combed ring-spun cotton, 20% recycled polyester
  • Dense brushed 3-end fleece with a 100% organic cotton face
  • Heavyweight fabric · oversized fit

Quarter Zip, Zip Up & Heavyweight Hoodie

Care

  • Machine wash cold, inside out, with like colours
  • Do not iron directly on the print or embroidery
  • Tumble dry low or hang to dry
  • Do not dry clean or bleach

Following these steps keeps your piece looking sharp for longer.
